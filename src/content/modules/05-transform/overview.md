# Transform & Perspective Matching

Welcome to Module 5—the final technical module! Master perspective and transformation to make head replacements geometrically perfect.

## What You'll Learn

- **Perspective Fundamentals**: Understand vanishing points and perspective in composites
- **Transform Tools**: Free Transform, Perspective, Distort, Warp
- **Puppet Warp**: Organic transformations for natural adjustments
- **Vanishing Point Tool**: Match architectural and geometric perspective
- **Head Replacement Workflow**: Size, angle, and perspective matching for faces

## Why This Is Critical

Your satirical project requires:
- 47 heads matched to 47 body positions/angles
- Each head correctly sized for its figure
- Each head angled to match body orientation
- Perspective matching (distance figures smaller, correct viewing angle)
- Natural appearance (no floating heads, proper neck integration)

Poor perspective = obvious fake, immediately visible.

## Module Structure

1. **4 Exercises**: Perspective basics, transform tools, puppet warp, vanishing points
2. **2 Challenges**: Perfect head replacement, architectural perspective composite
3. **Assessment**: 10 criteria (80% passing)

## Time Commitment

Approximately **3 hours**. Perspective requires precision and practice.

## Prerequisites

Modules 1-4 complete (layer management, masking, color matching, blending).
