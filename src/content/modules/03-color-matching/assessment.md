# Module Assessment: Color Matching & Correction

Complete this self-assessment to verify your color matching mastery.  Be honest—color matching makes or breaks composites.

## Assessment Criteria

**Task**: Identify color temperature (warm/cool/neutral) in 5 different images and explain what creates that temperature.

**Success Criteria**:

- [ ] Can use eyedropper to verify analysis (RGB ratios)

## Color Matching Principles

Remember:

1. **Observation first**: Analyze before adjusting
2. **Subtlety wins**: Many small adjustments > one huge adjustment
3. **Context matters**: Colors are relative to their environment
4. **Consistency is key**: 47 heads need uniform approach
5. **Skin tones are critical**: Human eyes are unforgiving
6. **Temperature before saturation**: Get warm/cool right first
7. **Split-toning adds dimension**: Don't make everything uniform
8. **Test on multiple displays**: If possible
9. **Save presets**: Reuse successful adjustments
10. **Iterate**: Match → evaluate → refine → repeat
