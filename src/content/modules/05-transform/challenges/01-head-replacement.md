# Challenge: Perfect Head Replacement

## Objective

Demonstrate complete head replacement mastery: extract head, match to body perfectly in size/angle/perspective, integrate seamlessly.

## Requirements

- Source: Portrait with head to replace
- Target: Full figure (painting or photo) where head will be replaced
- Must demonstrate ALL previous module skills

## Tasks

1. **Extract head cleanly** (Module 2 skills)
2. **Position and scale** to match body
3. **Rotate and transform** to match body angle
4. **Match perspective** if needed
5. **Color match** to body/environment (Module 3)
6. **Blend neck seam** perfectly
7. **Add shadows/highlights** (Module 4)
8. **Match texture** to painting/photo style
9. **Final integration** polish

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Head size matches body proportions
- [ ] Head angle matches body orientation
- [ ] Perspective correct (viewing angle)
- [ ] Neck seam invisible
- [ ] Colors match body/environment
- [ ] Lighting matches (shadows/highlights)
- [ ] Texture consistent
- [ ] Looks like original, not composite
- [ ] Professional quality
- [ ] Completed under 60 minutes

## Evaluation

90-100% = Perfect head replacement, indistinguishable from original
75-89% = Good with minor issues
<75% = Needs significant work

This IS your satirical project—practice on 1, execute on 47.
