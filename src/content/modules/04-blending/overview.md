## Key Concepts

### Blend Modes
How layers interact mathematically with layers below. Each mode creates different visual effects.

### Light and Shadow
Objects exist in space through light interaction:
- Cast shadows: Object blocks light
- Contact shadows: Where object meets surface
- Highlights: Where light hits directly
- Ambient occlusion: Crevices stay dark

### Integration
Subjects must "belong" in their environment through:
- Appropriate shadows
- Edge lighting from environment
- Color contamination from surroundings
- Matched texture and detail level
