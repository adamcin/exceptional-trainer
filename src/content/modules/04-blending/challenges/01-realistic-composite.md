# Challenge: Build Photorealistic Composite

## Objective

Create a complete, photorealistic composite demonstrating all Module 4 skills: shadows, highlights, lighting integration, and texture matching.

## Requirements

- Subject (portrait) from different source than background
- Background (historical painting style preferred)
- Must include: shadows, highlights, lighting adjustments, texture matching

## Tasks

1. **Extract and Position**: Clean mask, appropriate placement
2. **Color Match**: Use Module 3 skills to match colors
3. **Create Shadows**: Cast shadow + contact shadow
4. **Add Highlights**: Edge lighting from environment
5. **Lighting Integration**: Dodge/burn to match environment lighting
6. **Texture Matching**: Add grain/texture to match background
7. **Final Polish**: Unified treatment

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Subject appears to belong in environment
- [ ] Shadows are realistic and correctly positioned
- [ ] Lighting direction matches environment
- [ ] Texture/grain consistent throughout
- [ ] Passes glance test (looks real initially)
- [ ] Quality holds at high magnification
- [ ] Professional portfolio quality
- [ ] Completed under 60 minutes

## Evaluation: 90-100% = Photorealistic, 75-89% = Good with minor issues, <75% = Needs work

## Time: 60 minutes
