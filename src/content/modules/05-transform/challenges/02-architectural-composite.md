# Challenge: Architectural Perspective Composite

## Objective

Composite subject into architectural scene with perfect perspective matching.

## Requirements

- Architectural background with clear perspective (building interior, street, room)
- Subject to composite
- Must match perspective exactly

## Tasks

1. Identify vanishing points in background
2. Transform subject to match perspective
3. Ensure subject's implied lines converge to same vanishing points
4. Scale appropriately for position in space
5. Add appropriate shadows matching light direction
6. Integrate fully

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Subject matches background perspective perfectly
- [ ] Scale appropriate for position
- [ ] No geometric inconsistencies
- [ ] Shadows follow perspective
- [ ] Fully integrated
- [ ] Looks geometrically correct
- [ ] Completed under 50 minutes

## Time: 50 minutes
