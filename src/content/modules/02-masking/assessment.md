## Masking Principles to Remember

As you move forward:

1. **Tool selection matters**: No single tool solves every problem
2. **Zoom is essential**: Work at 100%+ for edge quality
3. **Multiple passes win**: Rough → Refine → Perfect
4. **Test on multiple backgrounds**: Black, white, gray, colored
5. **Edge character varies**: Hard where hard, soft where soft
6. **Hair needs patience**: The hardest skill, worth mastering
7. **Channels are powerful**: Don't forget this advanced technique
8. **Context matters**: Optimize mask for its destination
9. **Quality compounds**: Poor mask + good color correction = still poor
10. **Practice develops speed**: Quality + speed = professional workflow

## Masking Quality Checklist

Professional-quality masks exhibit:
- ✓ No halos on any background color
- ✓ No color fringing or contamination
- ✓ Fine detail preserved (hair strands visible)
- ✓ Edge character appropriate (hard/soft where needed)
- ✓ Smooth, non-aliased curves
- ✓ Consistent quality around entire perimeter
- ✓ Works believably in composite scenarios
- ✓ Invisible at normal viewing distance
- ✓ Holds up at high magnification (200%+)
- ✓ Efficient workflow (balance quality and speed)
