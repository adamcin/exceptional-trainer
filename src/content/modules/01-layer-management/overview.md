# Advanced Layer Management

Welcome to the first module of your Photoshop mastery journey! This module focuses on the foundation of professional compositing: advanced layer management.

## What You'll Learn

In this module, you'll master the essential organizational and structural techniques that professional compositors rely on:

- **Complex Layer Hierarchies**: Learn to organize dozens or hundreds of layers efficiently
- **Smart Objects**: Understand non-destructive editing workflows that protect your original work
- **Layer Groups and Comps**: Create reusable variations and maintain clean project structure
- **Blending Modes**: Apply the right mode for seamless integration
- **Naming Conventions**: Adopt professional naming strategies for large projects

## Why This Matters

Your satirical "Declaration of Independence" project will require replacing multiple heads across a complex historical painting. Each head replacement involves:

- Multiple layers per subject (base, adjustments, masks, effects)
- Coordinated organization to track which figure is which
- Non-destructive workflows to allow revisions
- Grouped elements to maintain sanity in a 50+ layer document

Without strong layer management skills, you'll quickly lose track of your work and struggle to make changes.

## Module Structure

1. **Exercises**: Three guided exercises building your foundational skills
2. **Challenges**: Two application challenges testing your abilities
3. **Assessment**: Self-assessment to verify you've mastered the concepts

## Time Commitment

This module takes approximately **3 hours** to complete. Take your time—quality matters more than speed.

## Prerequisites

This is the foundation module, so no prior Photoshop experience beyond basic familiarity is required. You should know how to:

- Open Photoshop
- Create a new document
- Use basic navigation (zoom, pan)
- Open the Layers panel
